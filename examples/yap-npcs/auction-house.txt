# Auction House NPC — opens the shared /ah GUI.
# Requires yap-npcs + yap-playerdata (features.auctions + economy) + yap-essentials.
#
# Players can browse listings, buy with click, and sell via /ah sell <price>
# (or the Sell button hint in the GUI).

/npc create auction_house Auction House
/npc setplayer auction_house ah
/npc setdialogue auction_house &6Auction House&7 — buy from the board, sell held items, manage your listings.
/npc respawn
