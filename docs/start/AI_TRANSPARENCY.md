# AI transparency

**Document ID:** `YAP-AI-001`  
**Effective:** 2026-09-13  
**Applies to:** YaPcore first-party source, docs, scripts, and release packaging in this repository.

YapLabs discloses the use of **artificial intelligence (AI) coding assistants** in the design, implementation, documentation, and maintenance of YaPcore. This page states what that means for operators, contributors, and downstream redistributors.

---

## Summary

| Topic | Position |
|-------|----------|
| **Was AI used?** | **Yes** — AI assistants (including Cursor / Composer-class tools) have been used extensively to draft, refactor, document, and review YaPcore. |
| **Who is accountable?** | **YapLabs / human maintainers** — AI does not replace ownership, review, or release sign-off. |
| **Is AI “the author”?** | **No.** Copyright and license obligations attach to the human contributors and the project under [GPLv3](../../LICENSE). |
| **Does AI change the license?** | **No.** First-party YaPcore remains [GNU GPLv3](LICENSING.md) (or later) unless a file states otherwise. |
| **Are secrets fed to AI?** | **Must not be.** Contributors must not paste production secrets, private keys, or customer data into AI tools — [SECRETS.md](SECRETS.md) · [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md). |

---

## Scope of AI assistance

AI tools may have been used for, among other tasks:

- Implementing and refactoring chassis, Link, Folia-adjacent, and first-party plugin code
- Splitting large classes to meet the **≤500-line domain** rule ([CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md))
- Drafting and revising operator documentation, whitepapers, and release notes
- Suggesting tests, scripts, packaging, and CI-adjacent glue
- Explaining protocols, upstream Folia/Geyser/Cloudburst references, and migration plans

AI output is treated as **untrusted draft material** until a human maintainer accepts it into the tree.

---

## Human accountability

1. **Review** — Maintainers are expected to review AI-assisted changes for correctness, security, threading (Folia SYNC/HEAVY/UI), licensing, and honesty of product claims.
2. **Verification** — Ship gates remain human-owned: compile, domain line limits, tests, soak/MSPT cites, and release packaging ([PRODUCTION_READY.md](../ops/PRODUCTION_READY.md)).
3. **Attribution** — Git history records human committers. AI tools are **assistants**, not co-authors on the copyright notice, unless a contributor explicitly chooses otherwise in a commit message.
4. **No warranty inflation** — Use of AI does **not** create additional warranties. Software is provided under GPLv3 terms as-is.

---

## What we do *not* claim

- That every line was written without AI assistance
- That AI “certified” security, Folia correctness, or Bedrock protocol parity
- That upstream Mojang / PaperMC / Cloudburst / Geyser materials were generated by YaP AI (those remain third-party)

---

## Contributors

If you use AI when contributing:

1. Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) and the PR template.
2. Do **not** include secrets, private keys, or production configs in prompts or diffs.
3. Prefer disclosing non-trivial AI assistance in the PR description when it materially shaped the change (optional but appreciated for auditability).
4. You remain responsible for the patch you submit — license grant, originality vs third-party code, and technical quality.

---

## Operators & redistributors

Running YaPcore does **not** require AI tools. Binaries and release zips are ordinary server software. Redistribution follows [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md) and the GPLv3; this transparency notice is informational and should travel with substantial documentation copies when practical.
