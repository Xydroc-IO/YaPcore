# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

YaPcore is an engineering product. Contributors and operators are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional in issues, PRs, and discussions
- Focus critique on code and design, not people
- Assume good faith; escalate privately when needed

## Unacceptable

Harassment, hate speech, doxxing, spam, or posting security exploits publicly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close, or ban for violations. Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
